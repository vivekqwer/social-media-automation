Clip Studio

Short-clip preparation and scheduled uploading for your own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Finds the most-replayed and most-discussed moments of videos you are allowed to reuse (Creative Commons or with the creator's permission).
  • Cuts and edits clips: trim, text, voice-over and captions.
  • Credits the original creator and copies the original tags and description.
  • Uploads and schedules the finished clips to your own YouTube channel after you connect it with Google sign-in.

Data and permissions

The service asks for permission to upload videos to, and read basic details of, the YouTube channel you connect. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

The dashboard itself is private and password-protected.
