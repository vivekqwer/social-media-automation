What it does for you

Shorts from videos you may reuse

It looks only at Creative Commons videos and videos whose creator said yes. Every Short credits the creator and links the original.

Your own videos too

Upload your own long video, or drop files in a folder on your PC. It cuts the best parts and writes the title, description and tags.

Automatic scheduling

Clips wait in a queue and go up at the best time, one a day or more — inside YouTube's daily limits.

Strike protection

If YouTube ever flags a video, that creator is never used again, waiting clips from them are held, and you get the steps to answer.

Helper PC

A small free program on your own PC does the downloads and keeps a safe copy of every video, so nothing is lost.

Alerts on your phone

A short message when a video goes live, when something needs you, or when a problem was fixed by itself.

Instagram and Facebook

Optional: each Short can also go out as an Instagram Reel and on your Facebook Page once it is public on YouTube.

English, Hinglish or Hindi

Titles, the first line of the description and hashtags in the language your viewers search in.

How it works

  1. Create your account. Your own private space — nobody else sees your channels or clips.
  2. Connect your YouTube channel with Google sign-in, and tell it what your channel is about.
  3. Check the first clips, then let it run. Approve each clip yourself, or let it upload by itself.
