Privacy Policy

Clip Studio · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Clip Studio is a tool that helps its owner prepare short clips from videos they are allowed to reuse (Creative Commons-licensed videos or videos used with the creator's permission), credit the original creator, and upload the result to the owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule. This policy explains what data the service handles.

Information we access

  • Google / YouTube account data you authorise: your YouTube channel ID and channel name, and OAuth access/refresh tokens. We request the scopes youtube.upload (to upload and schedule videos you approve) and youtube.readonly (to show which channel is connected).
  • Public information about source videos: title, channel name and link, licence, description, tags and captions of videos you ask the service to clip, used to give credit and to prepare titles, descriptions and captions.
  • Content you create in the service: rendered clips, text overlays, voice-overs, captions and scheduling settings.

How we use it

Only to provide the features above: connecting your channel, rendering clips, and uploading/scheduling videos that you approved or configured to auto-upload. We do not use YouTube data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers needed to run the service (hosting and database).

Google API Services User Data Policy

Clip Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Clip Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your use of Google data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in the service's database and file storage on its hosting provider. OAuth tokens are kept only while a channel is connected. Clips are kept until they are discarded.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Use My channels → Disconnect in the dashboard to delete the stored tokens for a channel.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Discarding a clip deletes its files. To have all remaining data removed, contact us at the address below.
